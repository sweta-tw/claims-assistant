POLICY ID: POL-1001

POLICY TYPE: Health Insurance

HOSPITALIZATION:
The policy covers hospitalization expenses when the insured person is admitted
to a registered hospital for medically necessary treatment.

MINIMUM HOSPITALIZATION:
Hospitalization must be for a minimum period of 24 hours unless the treatment
is specifically listed as a day-care procedure.

ROOM RENT:
Room rent is covered up to INR 5,000 per day.

PRE-EXISTING CONDITIONS:
Pre-existing diseases are covered after a waiting period of 24 months from
the policy commencement date.

EXCLUSIONS:
Cosmetic surgery, self-inflicted injuries, and treatments that are not medically
necessary are excluded unless explicitly covered by the policy.

CLAIM DOCUMENTS:
A hospitalization claim requires hospital bills, discharge summary,
diagnostic reports, prescription records, and a valid insurance card.
